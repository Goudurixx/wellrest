WellRest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

WellRest ("the app") is a rest timer that starts automatically between sets of a WHOOP Strength Trainer workout. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it. In short: everything stays on your phone.

What the app accesses

Notifications. With your permission (Android "Notification access"), the app reads the workout notification posted by the WHOOP app on your device. It uses this only to detect when a set ends and to display the name of the next exercise in its rest timer. Android delivers notifications from all apps to any app with this permission; WellRest ignores every notification that does not come from the WHOOP app immediately, without reading, storing or sharing it.

Settings. Your rest length and on/off preferences are stored on your device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not request internet access and cannot send any data off your device.
  • It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

Data retention

A short diagnostic log of the WHOOP notification text is kept in memory to help troubleshooting and is erased when the app's process ends. Settings remain on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Your choices

You can revoke Notification access at any time in Android Settings → Notifications → Device & app notifications (wording varies by device). Uninstalling the app removes all of its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.
